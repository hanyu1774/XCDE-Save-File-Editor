Party & Characters

Add or remove characters from the active party, and edit level, AP, and arts for any of the 15 playable characters. Changing the party roster directly is powerful but not fully tested by the community, keep a backup handy.

Careful with party membership. Adding or removing characters here edits the roster directly in the save file. Community testing has found this can occasionally cause crashes when opening the main menu if the resulting data is inconsistent. Save a backup copy before experimenting, and test in-game right after a change.
